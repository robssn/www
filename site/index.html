Frequently asked questions

How ROBSSN works.

Is ROBSSN already a live transaction marketplace?

ROBSSN is launching through structured commercial enquiries and partner onboarding. Marketplace capabilities will expand as inventory and partner coverage grow.

What can I submit?

Commercial vehicle, truck, equipment, fleet, dealer inventory and related service requirements can be submitted.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee an offer?

No. A submission starts qualification and routing; availability, pricing and commercial terms remain subject to the relevant provider.

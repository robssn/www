Frequently asked questions

Before you submit.

What can I submit to ROBSSN?

You can submit requirements for commercial vehicles, trucks, trailers, fleet assets, equipment, dealer inventory and related services such as finance, insurance, tracking, maintenance and logistics.

How does ROBSSN qualify an opportunity?

ROBSSN captures the opportunity type, location, timeframe, estimated value, requirements and contact details so the enquiry can be reviewed and routed appropriately.

Is ROBSSN a live self-service transaction marketplace?

ROBSSN currently begins with structured commercial enquiries and partner onboarding. Marketplace capabilities will expand as inventory and partner coverage develop.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee a transaction?

No. A submission starts qualification and routing. Availability, pricing, finance approval and commercial terms remain subject to the relevant provider.

Privacy notice

How ROBSSN handles personal information.

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Information collected

We collect the information you submit through our enquiry forms, including your name, company, email address, telephone number, location, commercial requirement and campaign attribution data.

Purpose

Information is used to assess, route and respond to commercial mobility enquiries; maintain business records; prevent abuse; and improve campaign performance.

Legal basis and consent

By submitting an enquiry, you consent to processing for the stated purpose. Where applicable, processing may also be necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a commercial arrangement.

Service providers

Form submissions may be processed by Formspree and related hosting or communication providers. These providers process information under their own security and privacy controls.

Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the commercial purpose, legal obligations and legitimate business records.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law. Contact sales@skunkworks.africa.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are applied, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

Changes

This notice may be updated as ROBSSN services and processing activities develop.
